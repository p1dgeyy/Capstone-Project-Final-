Data Privacy & Compliance

In compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and local governance policies of the City Government of Koronadal, please read and review the official Data Privacy Statement and System Terms & Conditions below.

1. Declaration of Policy

The City Government of Koronadal is committed to protecting and respecting your personal data privacy in accordance with Republic Act No. 10173, otherwise known as the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (DPA), its Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR), and other applicable privacy laws.

2. Processing of Personal Data

As authorized personnel operating within the PESO and CSWDO systems, you acknowledge that all citizen records, case files, beneficiary records, and identification information accessed through this portal are strictly confidential and collected solely for official assistance delivery and case tracking.

3. User Responsibilities & Non-Disclosure

Staff and administrative officers must uphold absolute confidentiality. Sharing official credentials, exporting unmasked contact records without authorization, or bypassing role-based access restrictions constitutes a direct breach of local government regulations and civil service rules.

4. System Logging & Audit Trail Compliance

All administrative actions, record updates, schedule modifications, and login attempts are automatically recorded with digital timestamps, role tags, and network identifiers for transparency, auditability, and regulatory compliance.

5. Data Retention & Erasure

Beneficiary and case records are retained in accordance with national archival standards and statutory retention periods. Archived records remain view-only to maintain verifiable integrity.

6. Security Safeguards

The City implements stringent physical, technical, and administrative controls including TLS encryption, Bcrypt password hashing, session tokens, and automated rate-limiting to prevent unauthorized access or system misuse.

7. Acceptance and Acknowledgment

By clicking "Proceed" below, you explicitly acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to adhere to these privacy obligations and operational standards during your entire system session.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I obtain administrative credentials?
Administrative and officer accounts are provisioned exclusively by the Head Administrator through the Admin Portal. Contact your department administrator if you need an account created or role updated.
What should I do if my account is locked?
Accounts are temporarily locked for 15 minutes after 5 consecutive failed login attempts. You can wait for the lockout timer to expire or use the "Forgot Password" link to initiate a verified reset.
Where do I log in as a beneficiary?
Beneficiaries must log in through the public beneficiary portal by clicking the "Back to Beneficiary Portal" link at the bottom of this page or navigating to official_login.html.

Administrative Knowledgebase

Security Standards & Protocols

All administrative requests utilize JWT authentication tokens and HttpOnly session cookies. Ensure that you never leave active sessions unattended on shared workstations.

Role Hierarchy & Segregation

The portal strictly isolates PESO operations from CSWDO cases. PESO Admins manage programs and evaluation rubrics, while PESO Officers manage beneficiary assignments and attendance records.

Audit Trail Guidelines

Every account update, program deactivation, or status change automatically logs the acting officer's credentials, timestamp, and action reason in accordance with city compliance standards.
