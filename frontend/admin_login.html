Data Privacy & Compliance

Pursuant to Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012), the City Government of Koronadal enforces strict protection over personal data processed across all municipal administrative portals.

PESO

Public Employment Service Office

Privacy Statement

PESO processes applicant and beneficiary information strictly for employment facilitation, livelihood development, skills training, and verified DOLE counterpart programs.

Terms and Conditions
  • Access records strictly within authorized administrative duties.
  • Maintain total confidentiality of applicant contact information and demographics.
  • All operations are immutably logged for audit verification.
CSWDO

City Social Welfare and Development Office

Privacy Statement

CSWDO processes sensitive beneficiary and household case data for emergency welfare assistance, social protection, and humanitarian grants.

Terms and Conditions
  • Respect privacy and handle case data with utmost discretion.
  • Follow social work evaluation standards and verify documentation authenticity.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I access my PESO or CSWDO account?
Authorized staff and administrators are assigned credentials by the IT Administrator. Enter your official username or email address and password to sign in.
What is the difference between PESO and CSWDO modules?
PESO handles employment, skills training, SPES, and job matching. CSWDO handles case management, emergency financial assistance, and social welfare programs.
What should I do if my account is locked?
After 5 consecutive failed login attempts, accounts are locked for 15 minutes for security. You can wait for the timer to expire, click "Forgot Password?", or contact your PESO Administrator.

Administrative Knowledgebase

Security Best Practices

Always use a strong password with at least 8 characters including letters and numbers. Never share your credentials or leave your session unlocked.

Role-Based Access Control

Staff members are restricted to authorized actions. All updates, evaluations, and disbursements are recorded with timestamps and admin identities.

Officer Password Reset Request

To change your official account password, submit a request to your Administrator. Once approved by the Admin, you will be authorized to set your new desired password.
