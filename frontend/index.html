Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register for an assistance program?
If you do not have an account, click the "Register New Beneficiary Account" button below the login form. Once registered, log in to apply for active PESO and CSWDO programs.
How can I track my application status?
Sign in to the Beneficiary Portal to view real-time updates on your application checklist, interview schedules, and release vouchers.
