---
trigger: always_on
---

1.Past Date Restriction  
System blocks scheduling on past dates; warning displayed if attempted.

2.Conflict Validation Restriction  
System validates date/time inputs to prevent overlapping or conflicting schedules.

3.Cancellation Restriction  
Cancelled activities remain visible with red labels; system records cancellation logs with timestamp and admin identity.

4.Certificate Distribution Auto-pull Restriction  
System auto-pulls eligible recipients from Training Records for Certificate Distribution activities.

5.Officer-Managed Beneficiary Restriction  
PESO Admins cannot edit beneficiary records directly from scheduling; assignments remain strictly Officer-managed with masked contacts.
